Perth Coastal Waters Forecast: Two Rocks to Dawesville, offshore from Rottnest Island

View the current warnings for Western Australia

Forecast issued at 1:01 pm WST on Saturday 14 November 2026.

Weather Situation

A trough and associated low pressure system extend from the northern parts of the South West Land Division, across the Gascoyne to the Interior. Meanwhile, a high pressure system entering the Bight tonight will weaken and move east on Monday, allowing the trough to extend into southwest WA. The trough and low pressure system will move east on Wednesday and Thursday, ahead of an approaching weak cold front.

Forecast for Saturday until midnight
