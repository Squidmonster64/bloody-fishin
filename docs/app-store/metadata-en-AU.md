## Description

Bloody Fishin is a decision-first fishing and small-boat conditions planner built for Australian anglers.

Start with a clear GOOD or POOR call, then inspect the measurements behind it: wind, gusts, swell, chop, water level, daylight and fishing conditions. Save the places you care about, add a custom spot, compare the coming days and share a forecast link with your crew.

Features:

- Decision-first current conditions
- Five-day and hourly measurements
- Conservative small-boat scoring
- Fishing windows informed by daylight, moon and water movement
- Saved and custom locations
- Last-successful forecast cache for weak connections
- Direct links to official Bureau of Meteorology information

Bloody Fishin is a planning aid. It is not an official forecast or warning service. Always check official marine warnings, local conditions and your vessel limits before departure.

Weather and marine model data: Open-Meteo and its named model providers.
