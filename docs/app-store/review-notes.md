# App Review notes — draft

Prepared: 8 October 2026 (AWST)

Bloody Fishin is a decision-support tool for fishing and small-boat planning. It combines live model measurements with repository-owned scoring logic and device-local saved locations. The app is not a simple website bookmark: it provides mobile decision views, custom/saved spots, caching and stale-state handling, multi-day/hourly inspection and native sharing.

No account or demo credentials are required.

Suggested review path:

1. Open the app and allow the live Fremantle forecast to load.
2. Inspect the Now decision and its wind, swell, tide/water and fishing measurements.
3. Open Days, then Hourly.
4. Open Locations, add a named custom location, select it, then remove it.
5. Use Share and verify the native share sheet.
6. Disable connectivity after one successful load and confirm the app labels cached/stale data rather than presenting it as live.

Safety context: this is a planning aid, not an official forecast or warning service. The UI links to official Bureau of Meteorology information and retains a visible safety disclaimer.

Forecast attribution: Open-Meteo and its named weather/marine model providers.

Reviewer contact name, phone and email: **required; owner to supply in App Store Connect**.
